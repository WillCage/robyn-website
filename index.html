About

I am a qualified Biokineticist passionate about helping people recover from injury, manage chronic conditions, and improve their overall quality of life through movement and exercise.

With a strong foundation in exercise science and rehabilitation, I work closely with each client to develop tailored programmes that address their specific needs and goals. Whether you are recovering from surgery, managing a long-term health condition, or simply looking to get fitter and stronger, I am here to support you every step of the way.

Outside of my biokinetics practice, I also work as a Personal Trainer at Fifth Gym in Stonehouse, where I bring the same dedication and personalised approach to helping clients achieve their fitness goals.

Services

Biokinetics

Specialised exercise prescriptions for rehabilitation, injury recovery, and the management of chronic conditions such as arthritis, diabetes, and cardiovascular disease.

Personal Training

One-on-one training sessions designed around your individual goals, whether that is weight loss, strength building, athletic performance, or general fitness.

Injury Recovery

Guided recovery programmes following injury or surgery, using evidence-based exercises to restore function, mobility, and confidence in movement.

Health Assessments

Comprehensive movement and fitness assessments to identify areas of concern and establish a baseline for your personalised exercise programme.
